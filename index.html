Privacy Policy

My Trips · Last updated 13 September 2026

Short version: I don't collect anything. My Trips has no server, no account, and no analytics. I cannot see your trips, and neither can anyone else.

Where your data lives

Your trips are stored on your own device and synced through your own iCloud account, in Apple's private database. That database is controlled by your Apple Account. As the developer I have no access to it, and no way to read, export, or recover it.

Calendar, Reminders, and Notes

If you give My Trips permission, it references items you already keep in Calendar, Reminders, and Notes so that a trip can point at them. It stores a reference, not a copy sent anywhere. Nothing is moved out of those apps, and nothing is transmitted to me. Deleting a trip never deletes your calendar events, reminders, or notes. You can revoke these permissions at any time in Settings.

Location

If you use the navigation button, your location is handed to Apple Maps to start directions. My Trips does not record, store, or transmit your location.

On-device intelligence

My Trips can pick travel details out of text you give it, using Apple's on-device model. That processing happens on your device. The text is not uploaded — not to me, not to Apple, not to anyone.

What I collect

Nothing. No analytics, no tracking, no advertising identifiers, no crash reporting, and no third-party code of any kind.

Children

My Trips is not directed at children, and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it.
